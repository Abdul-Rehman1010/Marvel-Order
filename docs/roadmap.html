Roadmap · updated 15 Nov 2026

Doom Flix, next level

A spoiler-safe Marvel watch-order tracker built around the countdown to Avengers: Doomsday. This is the plan to make it quicker to start, safer to use, and ready for the day the film lands and the days after it.

33 days to Doomsday · 18 Dec 2026

What we are fixing

  • The premise has a deadline

    The app is built around one date, 18 December, and nothing is designed for the day after.

  • Starting is slow

    Catching up on the 103 required checkpoints takes about a hundred separate taps, one at a time.

  • The list is buried

    On a laptop the first title starts roughly 1,400 px down the page, below a stack of dashboard panels.

  • Progress needs a safety net

    The ticking and un-ticking rules get automated tests and all-or-nothing saves, so a tap can never remove the wrong thing.
